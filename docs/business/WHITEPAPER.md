# WHITEPAPER — Wenits.fun (Product, non-technical)
Status: Draft v0.1 | Date: 2026-11-01 | Audience: Community + partners
Not an investment document. No token offering. No returns promised.

## 1. What is Wenits.fun
Solana Web3 entertainment ecosystem: AI hype radar, $10,000 fantasy trading arena, weekly leaderboard, X-gated community. One loop: scan hype → paper trade → climb ranks.

## 2. Problem
Memecoin discovery is noisy. Real trading punishes learning. Communities lack fair competition rails.

## 3. Solution (live today, dev mode)
- Moon Radar: live Solana trending pools, moon score 0-100, candles. Free, no login.
- Degen Arena: paper buy/sell with server-side fairness (anti double-spend, stale-price reject).
- Weekly board: top 100 by equity. Real data when DB live; sample labeled otherwise.
- X gating (optional): follow-to-enter, fail-closed, auto re-check.

## 4. What Wenits is not
Not financial advice. Moon score is momentum + volume signal, not prediction. Paper balance has zero cash value. No staking/swap/token live.

## 5. Economy (plain words)
Points track engagement. Rewards (future) are budget-capped and sponsored. Virtual cash never converts to real money. Any future on-chain feature will publish risks (impermanent loss, slippage, contract risk) before launch.

## 6. Roadmap (phases)
0 Foundation → 1 Core MVP (wallet, profiles, admin, points) → 2 Gamification → 3 Reward/token feasibility → 4 DeFi integrations → 5 Expansion. Phase gates enforced.

## 7. Risks (honest)
Market data depends on GeckoTerminal/DexScreener/X APIs. Prices can delay — trades block on stale feeds by design. Leaderboard needs settlement job (pending). Scraper sentiment not live yet.
